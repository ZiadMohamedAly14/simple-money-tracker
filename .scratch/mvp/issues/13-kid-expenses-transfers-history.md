# 13: Kid Expenses, Jar Transfers and history

**What to build:** A child records a Kid Expense from a Jar in about three taps and sees their full history. A parent moves money between Jars, and negative Jars after a sync are handled gracefully.

**Blocked by:** 12 (Kid Profiles, Jars and Top-ups).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Three-tap Kid Expense with Jar chips. It's blocked with a kind, bilingual message when the Jar can't cover it.
- [ ] Kids can edit and delete their own Kid Expenses.
- [ ] The parent can make a Jar Transfer from the Parent Area.
- [ ] History lists Top-ups, Jar Transfers and Kid Expenses, newest first.
- [ ] A Jar that goes negative after a sync shows its balance, blocks further spending from it, and is flagged in the Parent Area. A Seam B scenario covers this.
