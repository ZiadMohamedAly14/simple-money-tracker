# 17: Settings

**What to build:** An adult adjusts language, digit style and currency label, and signs out, from one settings screen.

**Blocked by:** 04 (Session lifecycle), 07 (Personal onboarding and Month overview), 11 (Parent PIN and Parent Area gate).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Language (per device, with the restart flow from 02).
- [ ] Numbering system: Western or Arabic-Indic.
- [ ] Changing the currency relabels amounts without converting them, and syncs as an Account setting.
- [ ] Biometrics for the Parent Area can be turned on or off for this device (from 11), shown only once a Parent PIN exists.
- [ ] Signing out revokes the session and clears local data.
