# MVP scope

## In

- Email sign-in with a one-time code; one Account per adult.
- Personal Budget: Expected Income, Categories, Category Budgets, Income and Expense Transactions, calendar Months, review of past Months, Income shown against Expected Income.
- Simple math (`+ − × ÷`) on adult amount keypads.
- Kid Wallet: several Kid Profiles, Jars (Save, Spend, Give by default), Top-ups, Jar Transfers, Kid Expenses, history.
- Kid-Mode devices with a Kid Session, Parent PIN and optional biometrics (offered after the PIN is set, switchable in settings).
- Learn section: about 8–10 lessons bundled with the app, in English and Arabic.
- Offline-first with multi-device sync.
- English and Arabic, full right-to-left layout, Western or Arabic-Indic digits.
- Delete account; archive a Kid Profile.
- Android builds through EAS.

## Out

Recurring top-ups or allowances, recurring expenses, notifications, any charts or reports (the category breakdown is a list), receipts or photos, several currencies per Account, payday-based Months, carry-over, Learn progress tracking, kid-requested Jar Transfers, analytics, data export, web app, iOS release, Google/Apple/guest login, background sync, linking Top-ups to the parent's budget, income categories, expense templates, savings goals, app lock, gold (XAU) as a currency, AI assistant, premium tier or billing.

## Later (in priority order)

1. **Kid Jar goal:** an optional name and target on a Jar ("Bike: 300 / 500") with a progress bar.
2. **Personal goals:** needs its own design, because Months have no carry-over.
3. **Month chart:** Spending by Category for the selected Month.
4. **Income categories:** label Income by source (salary, freelance) and show where money came from.
5. **Long time charts and reports:** trends across Months.
6. **AI month summary:** an assistant that summarizes a personal user's Month. It is the first feature that sends Transactions to a third-party service, so it needs explicit opt-in and a privacy-policy update.
7. **Expense templates:** saved quick-add presets (start with "repeat a past Transaction").
8. **App lock:** biometrics to open the app, as a settings switch.
9. **Gold (XAU):** gold as the Account currency; units and smallest unit to be decided.
10. **Premium and billing:** see `.scratch/premium/notes.md`.
