# MVP scope

## In

- Email sign-in with a one-time code; one Account per adult.
- Personal Budget: Expected Income, Categories, Category Budgets, Income and Expense Transactions, calendar Months, review of past Months.
- Kid Wallet: several Kid Profiles, Jars (Save, Spend, Give by default), Top-ups, Jar Transfers, Kid Expenses, history.
- Kid-Mode devices with a Kid Session, Parent PIN and biometrics.
- Learn section: about 8–10 lessons bundled with the app, in English and Arabic.
- Offline-first with multi-device sync.
- English and Arabic, full right-to-left layout, Western or Arabic-Indic digits.
- Delete account; archive a Kid Profile.
- Android builds through EAS.

## Out

Recurring top-ups or allowances, recurring expenses, notifications, charts beyond the category breakdown, receipts or photos, several currencies per Account, payday-based Months, carry-over, Learn progress tracking, kid-requested Jar Transfers, analytics, data export, web app, iOS release, Google/Apple/guest login, background sync, linking Top-ups to the parent's budget.
